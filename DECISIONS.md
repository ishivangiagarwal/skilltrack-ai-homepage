SkillTrack AI – Decisions

Question 1. Why did you choose this approach?

I chose React with Vite and normal CSS because they are simple, fast, and easy to work with. I focused on creating a clean and responsive homepage with a product dashboard, features, and clear call-to-action sections.

Question 2. What trade-off did you make because of the time limit?

Because of the limited time, I focused mainly on the frontend instead of building a backend or a real AI system. I prioritized the overall UI, responsive design, animations, and product showcase.

Question 3. Where did you use AI, and what did you personally verify or change?

I used AI to help with the React structure, CSS styling, and some UI ideas. I personally reviewed the code, tested the website on desktop and mobile, and changed the colors, layout, spacing, and animations to get the final design.
